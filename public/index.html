About Dr. Moturi

A thoughtful, science-driven approach to your hormonal health.

Dr. Hemlata Moturi is a board-certified Endocrinologist with Allegheny Endocrinology Associates, PC, practicing in Pittsburgh and Washington, Pennsylvania.

She believes the best endocrine care begins with a careful conversation - taking the time to understand each patient's history, lifestyle, and goals before building a plan grounded in the latest evidence. Whether you are managing diabetes, navigating a thyroid condition, or working through a complex hormonal issue, she partners with you to make the path forward clear.
